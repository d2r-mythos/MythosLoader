# MythosLoader

**Run several Diablo II: Resurrected clients on one PC — one per Battle.net account — and always know which window is which.**

[![Discord](https://img.shields.io/badge/Discord-Join%20D2R%20Mythos-5865F2?logo=discord&logoColor=white)](https://discord.com/invite/d2rmythos)

> **Status: in development. The first release is coming soon.**
> Join the [D2R Mythos Discord](https://discord.com/invite/d2rmythos) to hear when it's out.

## What it will do

- **Save your logins.** Log in once per account on Battle.net's own page. MythosLoader never sees your password.
- **Launch any mix of accounts** with one click: one account, a group, or all of them.
- **Run clients side by side** on one PC, with no extra tools to download.
- **Stay logged in.** Each launch saves the fresh login the game hands back, so the next launch just works.
- **Find your instance fast:**
  - name every game window (for example `D2R: sorc-main`)
  - press **Identify** to label each window on screen
  - press **Find** or a hotkey to bring one to the front
- **Skip the intro videos**, for all accounts or just some.
- **Live in the system tray**, with a menu to launch and focus clients.
- **Custom game options**, for all accounts or per account, with checkboxes for common switches.

## What it will never do

- Send one keystroke or click to several clients at once. Blizzard bans input broadcasting.
- Read or change game memory, inject anything into the game, or automate gameplay.
- Ask for or store your password.

## Requirements (planned)

- Windows 10 (version 1809 or newer) or Windows 11, 64-bit
- Diablo II: Resurrected installed through Battle.net
- No installer and no administrator rights needed
